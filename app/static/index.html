How it works

Four explicit layers.

01

Derive

PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 turns the password and random salt into a Fernet key.

02

Encrypt

Authenticated encryption protects confidentiality and detects a damaged or incorrect payload.

03

Transform

An exact 2×2 integer Haar transform exposes high-frequency coefficients without floating-point drift.

04

Permute

Password-derived ordering scatters payload bits across stable carrier blocks after a small fixed header.

Important boundary

This is an educational steganography implementation, not a replacement for audited encryption, secure transport, key management, or formal threat modeling. Converting the output to JPEG or editing it can destroy the hidden data.
